# 🎯 VEXSTRIKE

A fast, free browser shooter with colorful voxel-style maps. Fight smart bots, collect loot, and unlock new weapons. It runs on computers, phones and tablets with nothing to install.

## ▶ Play now
**https://vexstrike.netlify.app**

## Features
- **6 maps:** Crate Yard, Neon Rooftops, Frost Fortress, Lunar Base (low gravity), Desert Ruins (hidden loot boxes) and Factory (conveyor belts and cranes)
- **18 weapons** to unlock in the Weapon Store: rifles, shotguns, a sniper, a minigun, a rocket launcher, a railgun and more
- **Smart bots** that patrol, take cover, flank and take turns attacking
- **Coins and loot:** kills drop coins and ammo, and hidden loot boxes give bonuses
- **Profile:** pick an avatar, track your kills, deaths and matches, and unlock rank titles up to *Voxel Legend*
- **Touch controls** for phones and tablets: hold FIRE and slide your thumb to aim
- Your progress is saved in your browser
- Online multiplayer is available with public and private lobbies

## Controls
**Computer:** WASD move · Shift sprint · Space jump · Click shoot · Right-click aim · R reload · 1-9, Q, E or mouse wheel switch weapons · B weapon store · Esc pause

**Phone / tablet:** left thumb moves, right thumb looks. Hold FIRE and slide the same thumb to aim and shoot at once. Use the buttons for jump, aim, reload and swap. ☰ pauses and 🛒 opens the store.

## Run it yourself
Open `index.html` in a browser. It needs an internet connection, because it loads the 3D engine from the internet.

## Built with
- [three.js](https://threejs.org/) for the 3D graphics
- Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, all in one file
